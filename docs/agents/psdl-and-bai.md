# PSDL rooms and BAI

Read the [repository instructions](../../AGENTS.md) first. Paths and command examples below are relative to this repository root unless another working directory is specified. Builder CLI and self-test commands run from the builder root.

Also read [Exporter workflow and engine limits](exporting.md) and [The PSDL exporter](../exporter.md), which describes the parts, the rooms and the BAI model. Stock tram/train facts are in [Trains and boats](trains-and-boats.md#stock-data); texture conventions are in [Decals, textures and maps](decals-textures-and-maps.md#native-texture-reference).

## PSDL attributes as custom elements (removed)

The core had custom element types for the PSDL room attributes, the rooms, groups of building parts and the BAI roads and intersections (`creatable: false`, made by the `toPsdl`, `roomPerimeter` and triangle fan converters), with their exporter support (`custom_elements.py`, the mapping in `custom_element_conversion.py`), Lua (`psdl.lua`) and API self-tests. They were removed on 2026-11-06 with the exporter refactoring, to be redone later: the git history before that date has them. The exporter ignores such elements in older cities (how the builder loads elements of a missing type wasn't checked), and exports the roads and intersections they replaced (ghosts) again.

## BAI and pedestrians

**Paths:** the BAI uses the paths drawn in the editor (traffic lanes, pedestrian paths, cable car rails, see [The PSDL exporter](../exporter.md#the-bai)). The pedestrian paths are on the sidewalk surface, 7.5 cm above the stock convention (the sidewalk centre midway between the curb at road level and the outer edge); the rails follow the side of their lanes (the old derived rails were always on +x, which a few test roads have on the left side).

**Pedestrians (BAI sidewalks):** the walk target is evaluated 6 m ahead on an **unclamped** Hermite curve, so short sidewalk segments that turn make pedestrians fly off and circle. Segments touching a sharp corner need to be ≥ ~16 m. `bai_writer.spread_corner_points` moves only the sidewalk points; the 2 m car sections stay.

**BAI:** coordinates are (x, up, z) with x mirrored. The engine's room lookup returns the first intersection of a room before its roads, so don't put extra intersections in road rooms. For stock BAI derivation rules see the maintainer's psdl-viewer project, `Scripts/Formats/BaiFile.cs` (location in the builder's `CLAUDE.local.md`).

## Ambient car turns

Reverse-engineered from `midtown2.exe` (SHA256 `992c53c9…`, the same as the cable cars) on 2026-11-20, not checked in game. `exporters/psdl/traffic/ambient.py` emulates it on the BAI as written (`Route` of the BAI writer), and `checks/trafficPaths` (the *Show traffic paths* button of the intersections) draws it. The ambient car's rail set (aiRailSet, at +0x10 of the vehicle) has: +0x2c lane, +0x3c road, +0x40 side; +0x2e, +0x38, +0x42 the same for the next road; +0x4 the front half length h; +0x24 a sideways offset.

- **aiPath fields used:** +0x0 and +0x4 the index of the road in the intersection at its end and at its start; +0x118 and +0x13c the intersections at the end and the start (0x24-byte end records); +0x11e and +0x142 the end rules (the low half of the `uint` rule of the file); +0x120 and +0x144 a runtime flag word (the file's high half, always 0); +0xc the BAI flags. Side 1 is the second side of the file (start to end, arriving at the end), the other one the first side (end to start). A road of a slot leaves the intersection on side 1 when its start (+0x13c) is that intersection. A side has cars when bit 0 of its ambient type (+0x40, +0xa4) is clear.
- **Dispatcher** `539750` (called when a car is placed, `53a2ae`, and when it reaches the end of its road, `56bc67`): flag word 3 → `5396a0` (the road 2 slots further, lane clamped), 1 → `538ec0`. The only writer of the flag word is the traffic light set init `53c180`, which sets 3 on every road of a 4-road intersection when its two counts (`549d60`, `549d90`) are both 4; it wasn't worked out when that happens in a normal city. Otherwise, by the BAI flags and the lane (lane 0 is next to the centre line, the last one is the outer lane):
  - freeway, outer lane → `5399a0`: picks `539870` or `538ec0` at random, then overwrites the result with the lane routine below;
  - freeway, other lanes → `539870`: the first road with cars going forwards in the intersection's list that is a freeway, **same lane number, unchecked** (see the 0x5440c5 crash in [exporting](exporting.md)); without one, the lane routine;
  - 1 lane → `538a10`: every other road with cars going forwards (random), unless a freeway road comes first in that order (taken, in its outer lane). The next lane is the outer one when `558270` says the next road turns right by more than 0.5 rad (atan2 of its first centre segment on the car's x and z axes), otherwise lane 0;
  - lane 0 → `538bd0`: going **backwards** in the list, the first road with cars and the next one with cars, the second only if the start of its lane with the car's lane number (clamped only when greater than the count, so the count itself reads the sidewalk) isn't 2 m or more to the right of the end of the car's lane; random between the two, next lane 0;
  - outer lane → `538fa0`: the same going forwards, the second only if not 2 m or more to the left; next lane the outer one;
  - other lanes → `5392b0`: when 3 or more other roads let cars leave (`54a060`), the second road with cars going forwards (straight on), lane clamped. Otherwise the roads going forwards with 3 or more lanes: one → it, same lane number unchecked; none → `538ec0` (the first road with cars going forwards, outer lane); several → the one whose outer lane's last vertex is closest to the car's lane end along its x axis.
  - Going forwards is the right turn first, going backwards the left turn first (the order of the BAI intersections, counterclockwise in the program's coordinates, see `BaiIntersection`). No routine returns to the car's own road (no U-turns), except through a road with both ends on the intersection. No road found: `No eligible roads identified to turn onto from road: %d.` in mm2.log.
- **Path** (the per-frame `5577a0`, from the ambient update `56bb27`; `556e60` is the same for placement and queries, but takes the end tangent with the current side, `557438`): the next lane is clamped to the next side's lanes (`557c6c`). The car leaves its lane on the last segment at (L − h) / L (`548b70`), L being the length of the last segment of **lane 0** of its side (`547360` on the side's distance array), and goes to the first vertex of the next lane along the cable cars' Hermite (`548cc0`/`548d50` tangents: −Z of the last section or +Z of the first for the leaving road, −Z of the first or +Z of the last for the next one, times |dx| + |dz|). Both points move by +0x24 times the section's x axis: 0.5 sin(2π r), set in the rail set init `556d50`. h is −min z of the car's body box (stored at `568004`, as for the cable cars): 1.6–2.6 m for the stock ambient cars, 3.7–6.5 m for the limousine, buses and truck; the check draws 2.3 m.
- **Left-hand traffic** (`[Ambients Drive On The Left]` of the AIMAP, read at `555dcf` into +0x4 of the object at aiMap +0x20; stock London has it): when the map loads (`535229`), `545d00` runs on every road. It copies the lanes of each side over the lane array of the other side, the last lane first and each one reversed (side A lane k becomes side B lane count_A − 1 − k), then recomputes the distances. The lane counts stay, and so do the routines above: the lane next to the centre line becomes the last one, so it turns right, and lane 0 is the kerb lane. Each copy has the length of the source side (its lanes plus the destination's sidewalks) but the destination array is sized for its own lanes, so with different counts the lanes past the shorter side keep their own geometry and the copy writes past the shorter array. Stock London's one-way roads (0 lanes on one side) do this too, so its effect isn't known; the check warns only for two-way roads with different counts.
- **Checks:** `python exporters/psdl/tools/_test_ambient_turns.py` (synthetic crossings built as the BAI model: the routines, misaligned lanes, freeways, one-way roads, the paths, left-hand traffic; under a second). The intersections list their road ends by increasing atan2(z, x) in the program's coordinates, so going forwards meets the right turn first. The check was also run on cities built with the API (`elements.check` with `trafficPaths`), not in game.

**BAI-only roads** (the `bai` road type, `"geometryless": true` in its settings, see the builder's `wiki/Core-Roads.md`): the same `sectionVertices`, traffic components (copies of the `trafficLanes` of `doubleRoad`/`singleRoad`, `dividedRoad` and the sidewalks, without meshes) and traffic parameters as `psdl`, with `hVal` 0 (the lanes and curbs at the height of the curve, where a PSDL road with sidewalks has its road 15 cm lower). `divider` is only none or divided (1, which the BAI sees as a divided road). `get_geometryless_bai_sections` takes `v6, v5, (v4, v2), v1, v0` with sidewalks and `v5, (v4, v2), v1` without them, the order of `get_road_sections_layout` on the mesh of a PSDL road; `exporters/psdl/tools/_test_geometryless_roads.py` checks it against a PSDL twin on the export of the `geometrylessRoads` API focus. Their rooms are the PSDL rooms under them (`resolve_traffic_rooms`), so a network of BAI-only roads over a single terrain patch puts all its intersections in one room, with a warning for each but the first: give each intersection its own room (a small patch under it). In game (2026-11-09) a BAI-only road meeting a PSDL road at 27° ended 45 m from the intersection centre (sized against all roads), behind an opponent arriving on the other road, which then circled there: the opponent check reports such turns (`opponent_turn_too_sharp`, see [races](races-and-state.md)).
